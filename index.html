Privacy Policy for Montagu AISearch Bar

Last updated: 20 November 2026

I, Sybe, built the Montagu AISearch Bar app as a free guide to Montagu, Western Cape, South Africa, for residents and visitors. This policy explains what happens to the information you type into the app and which other services the app uses. The app is independent and is not affiliated with Langeberg Municipality.

What I collect

I do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

What you type into the app

When you ask a question, the text of your question (and the earlier questions and answers from the same session, so the guide can follow the conversation) is sent over the internet to an AI service to produce an answer. The route is:

  1. The app sends your question to a small relay service that I run on Cloudflare.
  2. The relay passes it on to Anthropic, which makes the AI model that writes the answers.
  3. The answer comes back to your phone.

I do not keep a copy of your questions or the answers. Anthropic and Cloudflare handle the data as described in their own privacy policies (links below). Please do not type personal or sensitive information, such as ID numbers, passwords or health details, into the app.

Other services the app uses

To show weather, events and news, the app fetches information from these services:

  • OpenWeather for the weather and forecast.
  • Montagu.org.za for the events list.
  • Langeberg Municipality (langeberg.gov.za) for recent municipal news.

These requests do not include anything you type. As with any website or online service, the servers behind them can see technical details such as your phone's IP address when your phone contacts them.

The app also fetches a small weather icon image from OpenWeather.

Log data and crash information

I do not collect log or crash data from the app. If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may collect usage and crash information about the app under its own policies.

Service providers and their privacy policies

  • Anthropic (AI answers): https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Cloudflare (relay service): https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • OpenWeather (weather): https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy
  • Google Play (app store): https://policies.google.com/privacy

AI answers can be wrong

Answers are written by an AI and can be incorrect or out of date. Please confirm opening hours, prices and bookings with the business before you rely on them.

Security

I take reasonable care to keep the app and its relay service secure. Information sent over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other sites

Answers may include website addresses of local businesses. These sites are not operated by me, and I am not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their privacy policies if you visit them.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has typed personal information into the app, please contact me.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and changes take effect when they are posted.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, or want to report a wrong or offensive answer, contact me at sybe.starkenburg@gmail.com.
